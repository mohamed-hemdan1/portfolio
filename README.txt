1) Open js/script.js and fill SITE_CONFIG at the top (GitHub, LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook, project links).
   Empty links stay hidden automatically.
2) Optional: put a Formspree/Web3Forms URL in formEndpoint to receive messages directly.
3) Upload the CONTENTS of this folder (index.html must be at the root).
4) Keep google9a0a4bc736eda4b3.html in the root for Search Console.
5) After deploy, add to <head> in index.html (replace with your real URL):
   <meta property="og:image" content="https://YOUR-SITE/og-image.png">
   <meta property="og:url" content="https://YOUR-SITE/">
   <link rel="canonical" href="https://YOUR-SITE/">
