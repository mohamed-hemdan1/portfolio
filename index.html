I am a second-year Computer Science student at the Faculty of Computers and Information, Kafrelsheikh University, focusing on Cybersecurity. Concurrently, I am enrolled in the intensive Software Testing track of the prestigious Rowad Digital Egypt (DEPI) initiative.

Combining a cybersecurity background with software testing provides me with a much deeper perspective on system analysis and vulnerability detection, far beyond traditional functional testing. Where standard QA confirms that a system functions as intended under expected conditions, a security-minded tester uncovers how unexpected input, boundary anomalies, and edge cases can compromise data integrity.

I am drawn to Automation Testing because it merges my passion for programming—which began with Java and Object-Oriented Principles—with the methodical precision of the QA discipline. Automation does not merely accelerate testing; it ensures exhaustive, repeatable verification that strengthens application defenses against unforeseen regressions.
